**This revision supersedes the rejected Sootwing, Riftstrider, Oathgullet, Unbidden Hand and Griefneedle concepts.** The user's correction is the controlling brief: "the warlocks summon demons, these are just red animals. go for different types of demons, goatmen, devils, fiends, that sort of thing."

These five designs are recognizable infernal beings with humanoid bodies, demonic faces and distinct demonic archetypes. Each is a new summon identity, not a recolor, mashup or Arch-form of an existing summon. Demon identity must read before props or effects.

## 1. Ashhorn Hexer — goatman occultist

A broad, simplified crimson goatman demon with enormous black curled ram horns, a long angular goat face, tiny inset yellow eyes, a pointed dark beard and heavy cloven hooves. A compact hunched humanoid body has oversized long claw hands and bent digitigrade legs. One hand holds a sparse orange curse sigil; the other hangs low in a threatening gesture. A small plain charcoal waistcloth is its only clothing. No mantle, staff, fur texture, realistic anatomy or detailed ornaments.

Silhouette: massive curled horns above a compact demon torso, two unmistakable split hooves and one raised spell hand. Palette: broad crimson facets, charcoal shadow planes, black horns/hooves, tiny flat yellow eyes and one controlled orange sigil. Full-body three-quarter pose. Rendering should closely match the actual approved Imp and Pit Brute reference images, while preserving a distinct goatman identity.

Personality and potential role: a malicious covenant demon who imposes hexes and extracts pact payments. This is a concept, not implemented or balanced rules.

Identity guardrail: retain the curled ram horns, goat beard, split hooves and curse gesture. Avoid turning it into the existing Imp with wings removed; reassess torso/pose similarity before production approval. No wings, stone armor or magma seams.
## 2. Vexrath, the Red Advocate — aristocratic devil

A tall, lean true devil with crimson skin, a long angular humanlike face, hooked nose, swept upright horns and a controlled cruel smile. A long whip-thin tail ends in a large barbed point. He wears a sharply cut black infernal court coat with a high collar and broad restrained panels, open enough to show crimson anatomy. One hand rests behind his back while the other holds a slender dueling blade downward. His poise should feel predatory and disdainful rather than heroic.

Silhouette: narrow upright duelist, high collar, swept horns and one clear looping tail. Palette: deep crimson, ink-black, muted ivory and a tiny amber gaze. Three-quarter full-body pose with a clear blade silhouette; no complex filigree or jewelry clutter.

Personality and potential role: a contract-enforcing devil that singles out a debtor and punishes broken conditions.

Identity guardrail: fully demonic face and natural horns, not a human in a devil costume. No wings, huge fists, layered rock plates or imp proportions.

## 3. Gormaw, the Oath-Eater — grotesque hunger fiend

A vile potbellied fiend with a huge, sagging humanoid trunk, disproportionately short bowed legs and thick drooping arms. His oversized lower jaw carries two broad uneven tusks; tiny cruel eyes sit deep under a low brow and mismatched stub horns. His belly and broad flesh planes stay visible above a simple dark butcher's apron. One hand carries a single hooked cleaver, the other presses possessively against his belly. His face conveys sullen, ravenous malice, not cheerful comedy.

Silhouette: pear-shaped body, low center of gravity, enormous gut and jaw. Palette: dark dried-blood red, muted ash flesh shadows, black apron and yellowed ivory tusks. Three-quarter full body, with the cleaver clearly separate from the torso.

Personality and potential role: an infernal glutton that devours offerings and grows more dangerous through sacrifice.

Identity guardrail: soft, heavy and grotesque rather than muscular. Do not reuse the Pit Brute's square shoulders, rock gauntlets, clenched-fist pose, armored thighs or athletic torso. No animal muzzle.

## 4. Velissa, the Whisperer — infernal tempter

An uncanny female demon with an elongated angular humanoid face, ash-lilac skin, ember eyes and a black crown of two broad swept horns. Four long humanoid arms emerge clearly from two shoulder pairs, held in distinct persuasive gestures: invitation, warning, concealment and a curse sign. A high-necked torn ceremonial gown has a simple deep-crimson body and charcoal outer folds. Show slender clawed fingers and demonic feet beneath the hem. The expression is knowing, emotionally unreadable and dangerous.

Silhouette: narrow central body with four unmistakable arms and crown-like horns; poised, almost motionless. Palette: muted ash-lilac, crimson, charcoal and a small amber gaze. Three-quarter full body with open spaces between every forearm. Keep the face severe and the clothing ceremonial.

Personality and potential role: a whispering tempter who turns an enemy's intent against it through bargains and compulsions.

Identity guardrail: clearly infernal anatomy and presence, not an ordinary sorceress, animal creature, floating object or winged imp. Four arms must read cleanly rather than becoming a tangle. No busy ornaments, glowing aura cloud or oversized weapon.

## 5. Chainbound Scourger — executioner demon

A towering, lean executioner fiend with long crimson sinewy limbs, a narrow waist and a bald angular demonic head. Two thick horns curve down beside its head, framing a permanently clenched toothy grimace. Its throat sits inside a heavy black iron neck yoke. Broad manacles bind each wrist; a pair of heavy punishment chains drags from the hands. It wears a short ragged charcoal waistcloth, keeping the inhuman anatomy visible. It looks resentful, disciplined and eager to inflict punishment.

Silhouette: very tall inverted V, long arms and two weighty chain curves reaching the ground. Palette: dark burgundy flesh, nearly black iron, selective ivory teeth and subdued amber eyes. Three-quarter full-body pose; one chain held taut across a low diagonal, the other hanging in a simple open loop. Use large readable chain links, not many tiny details.

Personality and potential role: a demonic jailer that binds a target and exacts punishment when it acts.

Identity guardrail: lean, stretched and anatomical, not a second Pit Brute or Gorthak. No boulder shoulders, armored fists, lava seams, gigantic hammer or broad powerlifter chest. Natural demon head rather than a human wearing a mask.

## Shared visual brief

Serious Runelord 2D cel shading: large angular flat color regions, crisp hard shadow planes, exaggerated but readable proportions, strong silhouettes, purposeful expressions and very limited magical accents. Preserve quiet surfaces. No photoreal anatomy, painterly gradients, glossy PBR treatment, dense grunge, miniature decoration or cute mascot proportions.

Full-body three-quarter concept views on a plain neutral or transparent background. One character per image, isolated with clear margins. Show only the role-defining prop or two; identity must survive removal of equipment. Match the existing image family's rendering, not existing characters' heads, horns, armor or poses.

Each should read at about 96 px character height as a different demon archetype: curled-horn goatman hexer; upright noble devil with tail; pear-shaped tusked glutton; four-armed tempter; long-limbed chained executioner. Distinct posture, anatomy, personality and role matter more than color variation.
