# Runelord — Promo-video source package

Prepared 3 October 2026 for Claude Design. Open `index.html` to browse the clips and audition audio. This folder is intended to travel separately from the design-system folder; supporting art, fonts and audio are duplicated intentionally so an editor does not need the repository.

## Assignment

Make an exciting gameplay-led promo for the Runelord Warlock demo. Show real decisions, fire magic, summoning, defense, enemy projectiles, the Demon Lord and rewards. Use the supplied clips as gameplay evidence. Add editorial typography, transitions and carefully synchronized audio around that evidence. Do not invent gameplay or present generated concept art as recorded play.

The user specifically requests **Demon Warband starter-deck gameplay for every promo clip**, emphasizing summons. The title may show all three choices, but select Demon Warband. Fire cards are supporting art or spells within that run, not permission to feature the separate fire starter deck. Do not substitute older fire-deck captures.

Paste `CLAUDE-PROMPT.md` into Claude Design after uploading the package. `STORYBOARD.md` gives a suggested 45–60 second sequence; it is an editing proposal, not a transcript of an existing finished trailer.

## Contents

- `footage/`: silent 1920 × 1080 MP4 captures of the actual game interface, prepared from valid engine save histories for varied scenarios. These are curated demonstration setups, not a claim of one continuous unassisted run.
- `stills/`: still-image capture materials supplied by the recording owner.
- `replay-data/` and `title-capture.json`: capture provenance supplied by the recording owner. They are documentation, not production audio or art.
- `audio/music/`: four current runtime cues, with the GeneralUser GS license text. Explore, battle, boss and final-boss options are included so the edit can choose a coherent score.
- `audio/sfx/`: 25 selected original project-synthesized cues, plus source manifest excerpt and notes. Captured video is silent; these effects must be synchronized in the edit.
- `art/`: original hero/monster/summon references, fire backgrounds, selected cards, crown, brand image and licensed local fonts for titles/end cards.
- `supporting-asset-manifest.json`: hashes, source paths, roles and rights notes for the copied audio/art; it intentionally does not claim validation of captured video.

## Important boundaries

The user disliked the recent Warlock gold-trim recolor. This package uses the original production palette. Do not recolor/regenerate the hero for this promo. Use existing artwork and captured gameplay; no new character design is required.

Do not claim multiplayer, a completed campaign, other playable heroes or an imminent commercial release. Describe it as a playable Warlock demo. A safe final CTA is **Play the demo** with `https://wiltobuild.github.io/runelord/`. Verify that address immediately before publication if the edit is completed later.

Do not imply every scene is consecutive. Cut deliberately between moments. Keep UI legible long enough to recognize cards, summon entry, health/guard and boss mechanics. Do not replace visible card descriptions with invented rules.

The existing brand image may be a visual reference rather than a ready-made transparent wordmark. Inspect it before compositing. A clean typographic RUNELORD title in the included serif is an acceptable editorial treatment; do not distort a raster image into a logo.

## Rights and review

This package is the user's project material for Runelord promotion, not a new public stock-asset license. Font OFL and the existing GeneralUser GS license are included. SFX manifest states original synthesis with no external samples. Music rights notes preserve the existing source statement; no broader rights clearance is asserted. No third-party commercial music is needed.

Media integrity and capture-quality checks are documented by the capture owner separately. Supporting asset hashes are independently checkable. Neither successful decoding nor a copied asset implies user approval of a new edit. Deliver a reviewable draft before publication.
