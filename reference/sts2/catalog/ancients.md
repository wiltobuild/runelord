# Ancients & their relic offers

> Source: game-file extract from [spire-codex](https://github.com/ptrlrd/spire-codex) (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0). Raw JSON in `../data/`. Markup stripped; `(1E)` = energy, `(1★)` = Regent stars.

Ancients replace StS1's Neow + boss relics: one Ancient node opens each act and offers a choice of Ancient relics.

## Darv

Has a large pool of powerful relics. 50% chance to get 3 options from the pool, 50% chance to get 2 options plus Dusty Tome.

Selection: 50% chance: 3 from pool OR 2 from pool + Dusty Tome

- **Relic Pool** — Each set has one relic chosen at random. Sets with act conditions are only included if the condition is met.: ASTROLABE, BLACK_STAR, CALLING_BELL, EMPTY_CAGE, PANDORAS_BOX _(Excluded with Draft, Sealed Deck, or Insanity modifiers)_, RUNIC_PYRAMID, SNECKO_EYE, ECTOPLASM _(Act 2 only; 50% chance (vs Sozu))_, SOZU _(Act 2 only; 50% chance (vs Ectoplasm))_, PHILOSOPHERS_STONE _(Act 3 only; 50% chance (vs Velvet Choker))_, VELVET_CHOKER _(Act 3 only; 50% chance (vs Philosopher's Stone))_
- **Dusty Tome** — 50% chance to replace one of the three options.: DUSTY_TOME _(50% chance to appear as the third option)_

## Neow

Presents 2 positive options and 1 curse option. The curse pool is built first, then excluded relics are removed from the positive pool.

Selection: 2 from positive pool + 1 from curse pool

- **Curse Pool** — One relic is chosen at random from this pool as the curse option.: CURSED_PEARL, HEFTY_TABLET, LARGE_CAPSULE, LEAFY_POULTICE, NEOWS_BONES, PRECARIOUS_SHEARS, SILKEN_TRESS, SILVER_CRUCIBLE _(Single player only)_
- **Positive Pool** — Two relics are chosen at random. Some relics are excluded based on which curse was selected.: ARCANE_SCROLL _(Excluded if Hefty Tablet is the curse option)_, BOOMING_CONCH, FISHING_ROD, GOLDEN_PEARL _(Excluded if Cursed Pearl is the curse option)_, KALEIDOSCOPE, LEAD_PAPERWEIGHT, LOST_COFFER, MASSIVE_SCROLL _(Multiplayer only)_, NEOWS_TORMENT, NEW_LEAF _(Excluded if Leafy Poultice is the curse option)_, PHIAL_HOLSTER, PRECISE_SCISSORS _(Excluded if Precarious Shears is the curse option)_, SCROLL_BOXES _(Player has cards that can form bundles)_, WINGED_BOOTS, LAVA_ROCK _(50% chance (vs Small Capsule); excluded if Large Capsule is the curse option)_, NEOWS_TALISMAN _(50% chance (vs Pomander))_, NUTRITIOUS_OYSTER _(50% chance (vs Stone Humidifier))_, POMANDER _(50% chance (vs Neow's Talisman))_, SMALL_CAPSULE _(50% chance (vs Lava Rock); excluded if Large Capsule is the curse option)_, STONE_HUMIDIFIER _(50% chance (vs Nutritious Oyster))_

## Nonupeipe

Has a single large pool. Three relics are chosen at random.

Selection: 3 from pool

- **Relic Pool** — : BLESSED_ANTLER, BRILLIANT_SCARF, DELICATE_FROND, DIAMOND_DIADEM, FUR_COAT, GLITTER, JEWELRY_BOX, LOOMING_FRUIT, SIGNET_RING, BEAUTIFUL_BRACELET _(Deck has 4+ cards that can be enchanted with Swift)_

## Orobas

Offers one relic from each of three pools. Pool 1 includes a chance for Prismatic Gem or Sea Glass.

Selection: 1 from each pool (3 total)

- **Pool 1** — Includes either Prismatic Gem (33% chance) or Sea Glass (67% chance). Sea Glass picks from a random unlocked character other than the current one.: ELECTRIC_SHRYMP, GLASS_EYE, SAND_CASTLE, PRISMATIC_GEM _(33% chance (vs Sea Glass))_, SEA_GLASS _(67% chance (vs Prismatic Gem); character is random unlocked non-current)_
- **Pool 2** — : ALCHEMICAL_COFFER, DRIFTWOOD, RADIANT_PEARL
- **Pool 3** — Both relics require player setup. If neither is available, a locked option is shown.: TOUCH_OF_OROBAS _(Requires player setup (starter relic upgrade))_, ARCHAIC_TOOTH _(Requires player setup)_

## Pael

Offers one relic from each of three pools. Pool 2 has conditional additions and Pael's Growth has a reduced chance.

Selection: 1 from each pool (3 total)

- **Pool 1** — : PAELS_FLESH, PAELS_HORN, PAELS_TEARS
- **Pool 2** — The pool is doubled before adding Pael's Growth, giving Growth a reduced chance.: PAELS_WING, PAELS_CLAW _(Deck has 3+ cards that can be enchanted with Goopy)_, PAELS_TOOTH _(Deck has 5+ removable cards)_, PAELS_GROWTH _(Always in pool but at reduced odds (pool is doubled before adding))_
- **Pool 3** — : PAELS_EYE, PAELS_BLOOD, PAELS_LEGION _(Player does not have an event pet (Byrdpip relic or Byrdonis Egg card))_
