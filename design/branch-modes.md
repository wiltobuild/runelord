# Runelord mode ownership

Runelord is currently developed through two deliberately preserved modes that share the same combat, card, Warband, art, and audio foundation.

## Roguelike Demo — `main`

`main` is the deployed GitHub Pages demo. It is the immediate combat-first experience: Warlock gameplay, starter pacts, a nine-encounter Infernal route, rewards, items, the Soulforge shop after encounters three and six, and the demon-lord finale. Its future path is to add the other biomes and bring the three completed Coming Soon hero classes into gameplay, with each biome ending in a Sovereign.

## Conquer Mode — `conquer-mode`

`conquer-mode` contains the long-form campaign direction: persistent territory, repeated card upgrades, spell and Warband fusion, equipment, structures, defenses, invasions, open-world maps, Sovereign conquest, and faction relationships. It is intentionally separate while those systems mature.

## Eventual unification

These are not competing games. Roguelike combat is the foundation Conquer Mode will build on. The branches remain separate to preserve a stable deployed demo while longer-term progression systems are designed and integrated deliberately. See [campaign direction](campaign-direction.md) for the complete Conquer Mode vision and [the README](../README.md) for the current player-facing overview.
