# Runestones (items)

Runelord's "relics" are **Runestones**: carved stones, charms and trinkets, each glowing with one rune. Every reference item has been **renamed and restyled**. Effects are translated into Runelord terms, and about a third were reworked to involve the Warband. The `Restyles` column records the reference item it replaces. It is for development traceability only; strip it before any public build.

**Art direction:** 128×128 icons; a chunky silhouette with one dominant colour and a small glowing rune glyph. Tier is shown by frame/border (stone = common, bronze = uncommon, silver = rare, gold = boss/Elder, green = shop, purple = event). A little flatter than the reference: clean shapes, 2–3 shading tones.

Terms: Mana (energy), Guard (block), Might/Finesse (strength/dex), Exposed/Sapped/Brittle (vuln/weak/frail), Consume (exhaust), Fleeting (ethereal), Fervor (next-attack bonus), Inscriptions (enchantments), Hexes (afflictions), Inscribe (rest-site upgrade), Basic Attack/Basic Guard (Strike/Defend equivalents). Units = Warband units of any hero.
