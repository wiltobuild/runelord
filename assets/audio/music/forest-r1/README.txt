RUNELORD — LANTERNS & IRON
Three original loopable instrumental score mockups, set two.

04 Lanterns in the Hollow: forest exploration, 90 BPM, 4/4, E minor.
32 bars / 85.333333 seconds. Piano and flute trade a lyrical theme while
harp patterns flicker above restrained strings. Oboe leads the middle section.

05 Ironbound Heart: runeforge combat, 128 BPM, 4/4, D minor.
48 bars / 90 seconds. A 3+3+2 accent pattern drives low strings and piano,
with bells, broad brass and an energetic violin reprise.

06 The Silent Conclave: ritual / ominous exploration, 72 BPM, 3/4, C# minor.
32 bars / 80 seconds. Low choir, organ, sparse bells, solo cello and oboe
rise into a violin lament before returning to the sealed-door atmosphere.

FORMAT & LOOPS
All WAV files: 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo. Loop the entire file from frame 0 to
the exclusive end frame in loop_manifest.json. Standard WAV smpl metadata
also supplies a forward loop (its final frame is inclusive).
Each loop is warmed up through two prior cycles to carry sampled releases
and reverb into its start. A 2 ms correction removes endpoint offsets.
No fade-outs, padding, or separate intro. Use sample-accurate engine looping.
The audition page repeats using browser media playback, which may add a gap.

STEMS
The melody, texture and pulse stems share identical lengths and start times.
At unity gain their sum reproduces the mix. Play the mix OR the stems;
playing both doubles the music. Keep stems on one playback clock and ramp
gains for intensity changes. Reverb is baked in. Melody holds solo lines;
texture holds string beds, choir, harp, bells and organ; pulse holds bass,
ostinato and percussion. Not every instrument is present in every piece.

EDITING
Multitrack General MIDI files include tempo, time signature, section markers
and loop boundaries. JSON note lists contain final transposed pitches,
velocities, beat positions and durations. Replace the General MIDI patches
in a DAW for a different sound. MIDI does not include audio mastering or
the rendered reverb wrap. These are sample-based mockups, not live players.

CHECKS
Exported WAVs were checked for format, exact frame counts, matching stem
lengths, no sample clipping, audible first/last seconds, equal loop endpoint
samples and stem reconstruction within two 24-bit quantization units.
Details are in loop_manifest.json. No human listening or in-engine review
is implied by these technical checks. Full mixes peak at -1.5 dBFS.
