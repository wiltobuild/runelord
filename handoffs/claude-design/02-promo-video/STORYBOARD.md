## Editing principles

Deliver 1920 × 1080, 16:9. Match the capture frame rate reported in the capture metadata. Use clean cuts and occasional short dark/fire-accent transitions; do not obscure the attacks with constant flashes. Prefer three strong readable gameplay beats over many unrecognizable cuts. Preserve aspect ratio. Titles belong in quiet space and must not cover health, targeting, cards or the face of the featured character.

If a short format is wanted, make a separate 15-second cut from fire, one summon and the boss. Do not merely crop the full UI to vertical and lose the card hand and opponent. Vertical is a separately composed adaptation, not a required deliverable.

The package contains gameplay captures from curated valid histories. Use montage language, not a fabricated uninterrupted progression. Only show mechanics present in footage. No new boss eye overlay: it was deliberately removed in the game.

## Sound direction

The video files are silent. Start with one music bed, usually `02_oathfire.wav`, then transition to `demon-lord.wav` at the boss reveal. `01_ashes_of_the_first_rune.wav` is a restrained opening alternative; `03_the_crown_beneath.wav` is another boss option. Do not stack full mixes together at unity gain. Use short crossfades on musical boundaries and avoid cutting reverb tails abruptly.

Use `card-play.wav` on the visible card action; spell and summon cues at animation release/arrival; hit cues at contact. `ward-of-ash.wav` and `sinister-veil.wav` are sinister defensive textures. `fire-death.wav` belongs to the covering burst after a completed fire-enemy death, not the instant HP reaches zero. `boss-summon.wav` accompanies reinforcement arrival. Do not add a sound simply because its filename appears in the folder.

Keep dialogue absent unless separately requested. Suggested editorial delivery target: roughly −16 to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below −1 dBTP; these are suggested final mix targets, not measured properties of supplied files. Duck music briefly under major impacts and avoid heavy clipping/compression. Listen on headphones and small speakers before export.

## Draft acceptance

- Actual gameplay is the majority of the running time.
- Fire, all three creature types, a defense, ranged opposition, boss and rewards are represented when suitable captured moments exist.
- No cursor/loading/replay-reset artifacts distract from the edit.
- Cards and health/status information remain readable in key shots.
- Original hero palette is retained; no unsupported mechanics or release claims are added.
- Audio is synchronized, balanced and not distorted.
- End card URL and demo availability are checked before publication.
