# Runesmith cards (70)

Systems: **Deploy** (one card per construct type), **Charge / Overcharge** (at 3 Charge a construct uses its Overcharge instead of its action), **Discharge** (trigger Overcharge now), **Arc X** (lightning damage to a random enemy), **Static**, **Volts ⚡** (carries over, cap 10), **Scrap** (from destroyed or Recycled constructs, +2 each), **Recycle** (destroy your construct for Scrap), **Mech Up N** (pilot the Thunderhulk for N turns; Hull = 12 + 4×Scrap spent), **Piloting:**, **Calibrate X** (raise Thunderhulk Slam damage this combat). See [heroes.md §3](../design/heroes.md). Cost `1 +2⚡` = 1 Mana and 2 Volts.
