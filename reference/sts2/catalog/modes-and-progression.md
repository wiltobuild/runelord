## Ascension levels (cumulative)

| Lvl | Name | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No Ascension | Play without any Ascension modifiers. |
| 1 | Swarming Elites | Elites spawn more often. |
| 2 | Weary Traveler | Ancients only heal 80% of your missing HP. |
| 3 | Poverty | Enemies and Treasure Chests drop 25% less Gold. |
| 4 | Tight Belt | Start each run with 1 less potion slot. |
| 5 | Ascender's Bane | Start each run Cursed. |
| 6 | Inflation | Removing cards from your deck at the Merchant is more expensive. |
| 7 | Scarcity | Rare and Upgraded cards appear less often. |
| 8 | Tough Enemies | All enemies are harder to kill. |
| 9 | Deadly Enemies | All enemies have deadlier attacks. |
| 10 | Double Boss | Fight two bosses at the end of Act 3. |
